# Polar Fresh FMCG - Sales Performance Dashboard (2022–2024)

## Overview
This repository presents an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** for **Polar Fresh FMCG**, tracking sales revenue, unit volume, transaction history, and channel distributions across various product categories from 2022 to 2024[cite: 1]. 

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Revenue:** £19.95M[cite: 1]
* **Total Units Sold:** 3.8M[cite: 1]
* **Total Transactions:** 190.8K[cite: 1]
* **Average Unit Price:** £5.30[cite: 1]

### Core Insights
* **Product Performance:** Yogurt leads total revenue (£8.23M), followed by Milk (£4.10M), Ready Meals (£3.58M), Snack Bars (£3.40M), and Juice (£652.28K)[cite: 1].
* **Sales Channels:** Revenue is evenly split across retail channels—Retail (33.4%), E-commerce (33.3%), and Discount stores (33.3%)[cite: 1].
* **Delivery Fulfillment:** Deliveries taking 4 days account for the highest sales volume (£202.6K revenue / 770K units)[cite: 1].

## Tools & Methods

### Tools
* **Looker Studio:** Used to build, visualize, and present interactive dynamic reporting views[cite: 1].
* **Data Processing:** Cleaned, structured, and aggregated transactional dataset spanning 2022–2024.

### Methods
* **Dimensional Modeling:** Configured relational fields including Brand, Region, Pack Type, Category, Channel, and Order Fulfillment Days to enable dynamic filtering[cite: 1].
* **Visualizations Applied:**
  * **Scorecards:** Expressing top-level business metric aggregates (£ Revenue, Units, Transactions, Avg Price)[cite: 1].
  * **Bar & Column Charts:** Stacked bars for category revenue vs. delivered quantities, alongside horizontal distributions for fulfillment turnaround times[cite: 1].
  * **Donut Chart:** Displaying overall channel contribution shares[cite: 1].
  * **Time Series Chart:** Evaluating historical trends across categories from Jan 2022 to Nov 2024[cite: 1].

## Conclusion
The dashboard highlights strong overall revenue stability (£19.95M) driven significantly by the Yogurt product category[cite: 1]. Balanced distribution across E-commerce, Retail, and Discount channels shows balanced operational exposure[cite: 1]. Order fulfillment times show stable performance across 1–5 days delivery cycles, giving FMCG managers clear levers for supply chain and inventory optimizing strategies[cite: 1].
